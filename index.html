Cruise a first playable 3D slice inspired by Hyderabad’s HITEC City and Madhapur. Follow local signboards, pass custom street props, and collect checkpoint rewards on your drive.

01 / FREE ROAMAcceleration, reverse, steering, braking and camera modes.
02 / CITY DETAILSCustom signboards, pillars, flyover, safety barriers and flyer panels.
03 / EXPLOREThree named stops and a live minimap.
Prototype honesty note: this is not a complete inch-by-inch reconstruction of Hyderabad. It tries to load public OpenStreetMap road/building data for a bounded zone; if that fails, a handcrafted city keeps the game playable. Posters and many roadside objects are original game props, not verified copies of every real-world flyer or barrier.
